How to prepare with GTU previous-year question papers

Choose your course, branch and semester, then open a subject to find its available Summer and Winter exam papers. Check the subject code against your current syllabus before practising. Solve a paper with a timer, review the topics you found difficult, and compare several exam sessions to understand the question format.

About PaperOnBoard

PaperOnBoard is an independent, student-maintained directory of GTU question papers. Browse courses, branches and semesters, or search by subject code. Paper links point to third-party sources; availability varies. We are not affiliated with Gujarat Technological University.
